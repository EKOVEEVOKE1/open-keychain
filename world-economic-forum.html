About us

We are the international institution for public private cooperation. We connect communities of leaders around global issues through high-level meetings, research, initiatives and digital networks. We are non-profit, impartial and independent. We believe dialogue and cooperation between business, government and civil society can improve the state of the world.

    Seven in 10 women diagnosed with #ovarian cancer will die from it - a mortality rate that reflects not just the disease, but failures in the systems meant to catch it. This World Ovarian Cancer Day can and must be a moment of change. Global frameworks for breast and cervical #cancer are already reshaping how countries approach women's #health. Extending that progress to ovarian cancer - through shared referral pathways, diagnosis capacity and treatment access - could save 2.5 million lives by 2050 without requiring entirely new programmes. The economic stakes are equally clear. In 2023 alone, ovarian cancer cost 11 countries an estimated $70 billion in economic losses. This is a solvable problem, and the tools already exist. https://lnkd.in/eb5Tu78H
